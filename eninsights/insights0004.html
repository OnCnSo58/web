China Excavator Industry Distribution Map: Major Cities, Number of Companies, and Share Overview

China's excavator industry shows clear regional clustering. From complete machine manufacturing to core components and aftermarket parts, different cities and regions play different roles in the industrial chain. Below is an overview by industry chain layer.

I. Complete Machine Manufacturing: Six Major Industrial Bases

China’s construction machinery industry is mainly concentrated in Hunan, Jiangsu, Anhui, Shandong, Zhejiang, Fujian, Guangxi and other provinces, forming six major industrial bases: Changsha, Xuzhou, Changzhou, Jining, Liuzhou and Xiamen.

• Changsha: The strongest overall strength. In 2024, its construction machinery cluster scale exceeded RMB 250 billion, accounting for over 30% of the national market. Five companies (Sany, Zoomlion, Sunward, CRCHI, Sinoboom) are consistently among the world’s top 50.
• Xuzhou: Backed by XCMG’s full industrial chain layout, it receives four-star ratings across all four competitiveness dimensions.
• Changzhou: Known for core components, covering excavators, loaders, road rollers, as well as castings, forgings, structures, transmission and hydraulics.
• Jining: Complete industrial chain with a local supporting rate of 60%. It gathers 25% of the province’s above-scale construction machinery enterprises, integrating bulldozers, excavators, and road machinery.
• Liuzhou & Xiamen: Round out the six bases.

Competitiveness Gradient:
• First tier: Changsha, Xuzhou
• Second tier: Changzhou, Jining, Liuzhou
• Third tier: Xiamen
Overall Pattern: Complete machines strong in Changsha and Xuzhou; components strong in Changzhou; specialized leaders in Jining; overseas layout in Liuzhou.

II. Total Number of Excavator-Related Enterprises

According to Tianyancha data, as of June 2026, there were over 28,000 active excavator-related enterprises in China.

• Shandong Province: Over 4,100 enterprises (Ranked 1st)
• Anhui Province: Over 2,900 enterprises
• Zhejiang Province: Over 2,700 enterprises
(Note: These three provinces account for over one-third of the national total. This figure covers the entire chain, including manufacturers, suppliers, traders, and repair services.)

III. Component Supporting Industrial Belts

Hydraulic components are the most critical category. As of the end of June 2025:
• Jiangsu Province: Over 6,500 enterprises (Nearly 25% national share)
• Zhejiang Province: About 4,900 enterprises (18.5% national share)
• Combined: 43.5% of the national total.

Regional Highlights:
• Taizhou (Jiangsu): Over 1,300 supporting enterprises supported by national policies.
• Ningbo (Zhejiang): Over 500 enterprises in the pneumatic chain; 70 above-scale enterprises fully digitalized.
• Shandong Network: Weifang (diesel engines), Qingzhou (hydraulics), Linyi (tires), Jinan (precision casting).
• Jining High-tech Zone: Holds over 40% domestic market share in key components. Among 170+ above-scale enterprises, there are 3 national single champions and 9 "little giants". Over 75% of excavator components can be sourced locally.
• Wenshang County (Jining): Annual machining capacity of 120,000 tons, casting 200,000 tons, surface treatment 100,000 tons. Covers 19 major series and 1,500+ types, supplying Komatsu, Shantui, Sany, LiuGong, XCMG, etc.
• Linyi: Led by Shandong Lingong, attracting 200+ supporting enterprises with an 80%+ local supporting rate and 700+ types of materials domestically substituted.

IV. Aftermarket and Overseas Layout

Aftermarket demand is stock-driven rigid demand.
• Jining: 67 enterprises formed a micro-cluster for small excavator exports, generating RMB 2.6 billion in 2024.
• Shandong Province: Over 380,000 construction machinery-related enterprises clustered into chains.
• Overseas Layout: Sany (Indonesia lighthouse factory), Zoomlion (Turkey spare parts center, Hungary high-altitude equipment base), Sunward (Pune, India R&D and production base), CRCHI (Russia maintenance base).

V. Quick Table for the Full Picture

• Changsha | Complete machine manufacturing leader | Cluster scale RMB 250 billion+, national share 30%+
• Xuzhou | Complete machine full industrial chain | Home of XCMG, complete supporting system
• Changzhou | Core components | Strong in hydraulic components and other key supporting parts
• Jining | Bulldozers and hydraulic components | Local supporting rate 60%, 75% of components available locally
• Wenshang County | Engineering machinery parts cluster | 19 major series, 1,500+ types of components
• Linyi | High-end equipment supporting | 200+ supporting enterprises, local supporting rate 80%+
• Jiangsu (province) | Hydraulic component industrial belt | 6,500+ hydraulic enterprises, 25% national share
• Zhejiang (province) | Hydraulics and pneumatics | 4,900+ hydraulic enterprises, 18.5% share
• Shandong (province) | Full construction machinery chain | 4,100+ excavator enterprises, 380,000+ construction machinery enterprises

Reference Value for International Procurement

Understanding the industrial belt distribution matters because different categories require different regions:
• Complete machines: Look to Changsha and Xuzhou.
• Core hydraulic components/pumps/valves: Changzhou and Jining High-tech Zone.
• Undercarriage parts/structural components: Wenshang County cluster.
• High-frequency consumables (bucket teeth, filters, seals): County-level clusters in Shandong and Jiangsu.
