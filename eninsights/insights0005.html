Construction Machinery Trade Show Preview | Planning Factory Visits After the Show? A Guide to China's Excavator and Mining Wear Parts Industrial Clusters

Attending construction machinery trade shows in China is the most efficient channel for overseas procurement teams to obtain high-quality supplier leads. Inside the exhibition hall, buyers can quickly complete face-to-face preliminary screening.

However, booth space is limited. It is difficult for source factories to bring full sets of heavy-duty samples and complete casting and heat treatment production lines into the venue. For professional buyers, extending the trip to visit the parts industrial belts around the exhibition venue is the best supplement for verifying capacity and deeply understanding the processes.

So, where should you go after the show? How to efficiently plan a factory visit itinerary? Here is a practical guide based on the industrial belt layout.

I. Distribution of China's Excavator and Construction Machinery Industrial Belts

Before planning factory visits, it is crucial to understand the regional clustering characteristics of China's construction machinery industry.

Complete Machine Manufacturing (Six Major Bases): Changsha (cluster scale RMB 250 billion+, national share 30%+), Xuzhou (XCMG's full industrial chain), Changzhou (core components), Jining (local supporting rate 60%), Liuzhou (overseas layout), Xiamen.

Core Hydraulic Components Industrial Belt: Highly concentrated in Jiangsu (6,500+ enterprises, nearly 25% of the nation) and Zhejiang (4,900+, 18.5%). Among them, the Jining High-tech Zone has a market share of over 40% in key components, with 75% of components available within the city.

High-frequency Consumables and Structural Parts: County-level clusters in Shandong and Jiangsu are stable sources. For example, Wenshang County has supporting capabilities across 19 major series and over 1,500 types of components; Linyi, with Shandong Lingong as the chain leader, has a local supporting rate exceeding 80%.

II. Mining GET Wear Parts: Top Post-Show Factory Visit Destinations in the Yangtze River Delta

The highest-wear GET (Ground Engaging Tools) in mining conditions include: bucket teeth, adapters, cutting edges/wear plates, pins and bushings. Open-pit quarrying and earthmoving stripping consume these parts heavily. Finding wear-resistant replacement parts suitable for heavy rock conditions is the core demand of overseas buyers.

If you are exhibiting in Shanghai, the Yangtze River Delta industrial belts have excellent geographical advantages. We recommend focusing on the following two clusters:

✅ Guangde (Junction of Jiangsu, Zhejiang, Anhui · First Choice)•
Industry Features: Guangde Qiucun area is the core cluster for mining GET forged wear parts in the Yangtze River Delta. It has leading wear-forging enterprises like Tuoshan Heavy Industry, and multiple manufacturers with self-built heat treatment workshops. Specializing in mining bucket teeth, adapters, and pin bushings, it can customize wear-resistant materials for rock conditions, with mature foreign trade delivery experience.

Itinerary Advantage: Extremely close to Shanghai with convenient high-speed rail access. With proper planning, a same-day round trip is possible, making it ideal for procurement teams with tight schedules.

✅ Ningbo (Second Choice)•
Industry Features: Yinzhou Yunlong, Cixi Binhai, and Fenghua areas are old-established precision casting clusters for bucket teeth in China (including leading manufacturers that participated in drafting national standards). With complete part number inventories for all models, they are skilled in drawing-based measurement and white-label trial orders—perfect for overseas traders stocking multiple SKUs.

Itinerary Advantage: Frequent high-speed rail departures and a mature industrial belt. If time permits, you can also visit nearby supporting factories along the way.

III. Practical Planning and Implementation Suggestions for Post-Show Factory Visits

For your China trip, planning transportation in advance is the prerequisite for an efficient factory audit. Considering time and flexibility, here is the most reliable plan:

1.Transportation Connection: Overseas visitors cannot drive in China. There is usually no direct public transport from the high-speed rail station to industrial parks. The optimal approach is to communicate with your target factory in advance and confirm whether they can arrange station pickup. Ride-hailing is relatively scarce in county-level industrial parks, so booking in advance avoids long waits.

2.Time Confirmation: Before finalizing the itinerary, make sure to confirm working-day reception with the factory. Most domestic factories shut down production on weekends, making it impossible to tour the lines or meet technical managers. Reserve ample time for the tour, sample testing, and business negotiations.

3.Exhibition Synergy: When communicating at the show's parts hall, don't just collect brochures. Prioritize verifying mining rock condition samples, material hardness, and physical-chemical test reports. Prioritize locking in exhibitors from Guangde and Ningbo, and upon exchanging business cards, immediately confirm their willingness to receive factory visits after the show.

IV. Reception Tips for Domestic Source Factories

When receiving global mining procurement customers, it is recommended to prioritize preparing heavy-duty mining heat-treated samples and multilingual part number comparison tables. Proactively offering high-speed rail station pickup/drop-off services can significantly increase overseas customers' willingness to visit, helping them complete on-site inspections more smoothly and facilitating cooperation.
